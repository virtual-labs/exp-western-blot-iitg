### About the Experiment 

Western blotting is a widely used immunological technique for detecting and analyzing specific proteins in complex biological samples. In virology, Western blot analysis is commonly employed to detect and confirm the expression of viral proteins in infected cells, cell culture supernatants, or recombinant expression systems. The principle of Western blotting involves separating proteins by molecular weight using sodium dodecyl sulfate–polyacrylamide gel electrophoresis (SDS-PAGE), followed by transfer of the separated proteins onto a solid membrane, such as PVDF or nitrocellulose.
