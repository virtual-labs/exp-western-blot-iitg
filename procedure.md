### Procedure

#### Sample Preparation
- Collect virus-infected cells lysate and add appropriate volume of protein lysis buffer containing SDS and reducing agent.
- Mix gently and heat the samples at 95 °C for 10 minutes to denature proteins.
- Briefly centrifuge at 12000g for 5 minutes and keep samples on ice until loading.

#### SDS-PAGE (Protein Separation) and Transfer of Proteins to Membrane
- Assemble the SDS-PAGE gel apparatus and load protein samples and a pre-stained protein molecular weight marker into separate wells.
- Run the gel at appropriate voltage until the dye front reaches the bottom.
- Carefully remove the gel, activate the PVDF membrane by soaking it in methanol, and then equilibrate it in the transfer buffer.
- Transfer proteins from the gel to the membrane using a wet or semi-dry transfer system at recommended conditions.
- Incubate the membrane in blocking buffer (5% skimmed milk or BSA in TBST) for 1 hour at room temperature to prevent non-specific binding.

#### Antibody Incubation and Detection
- Incubate the membrane with primary antibody specific to the viral protein for 1-2 hours at room temperature or overnight at 4 °C.
- Wash the membrane 3 times with TBST to remove unbound primary antibody.
- Incubate the membrane with enzyme-conjugated secondary antibody for 1 hour at room temperature.
- Wash the membrane again 3-4 times with TBST.
- Add appropriate substrate solution (e.g., chemiluminescent substrate).
- Observe the signal using a gel documentation system.
- Record the appearance of protein bands at the expected molecular weight [2].
